OBJECTIVE
---------
The commercial direction changed after P0-S2A-WL (Tenant-Branded
Transactional Email Foundation) was implemented and validated: custom
tenant sending domains are DEFERRED as a future paid white-label
capability, not the default. Nemryn now owns the standard transactional
email sender for every organization; the organization's name stays
dynamic inside the email content only. This phase rolls back ONLY the
P0-S2A-WL architecture, preserves every validated P0-S2A driver-
invitation guarantee, and re-verifies the result end to end.

IMPORTANT PRE-EXISTING CONDITION FOUND AT THE START OF THIS PHASE:
before any action was taken this phase, the working tree was inspected
fresh (per instruction, not assumed from an earlier context) and found
to ALREADY have most of the P0-S2A-WL-only artifacts removed from
disk — the WL migration, the WL SQL test, the three WL-only TypeScript
files, and the WL architecture doc were already gone, and
`src/app/operations/drivers/actions.ts` was already back to its P0-S2A
(organizationName-based) shape. This phase's own work therefore
consisted of: (a) confirming that pre-existing state was complete and
correct, (b) restoring the one file the instructions explicitly require
to be preserved as historical evidence but which was also already
missing (`docs/reports/p0-s2a-wl-tenant-email-identity-foundation.txt`
— restored verbatim, with a superseded-note added at its top, per "Keep
historical completion reports intact... Do NOT delete"), and (c) fixing
the remaining files that had reverted to their PRE-P0-S2A-WL content but
therefore still carried the OLD, ALSO-WRONG hardcoded
`"Zenward Mobility <no-reply@zenwardmobility.com>"` fallback and the old
"Sent by Zenward Mobility on behalf of…" footer — both of which this
phase's own instructions explicitly forbid, separately from the WL
rollback itself. No `git reset --hard`, `git clean`, `git checkout .`,
or `git restore .` was used at any point.
