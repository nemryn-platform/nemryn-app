1. DEFECT (confirmed by reproduction)
======================================================================================================================
  The previous docs/reports/NP-FIN-P1G-emergency-reversal.sql ran its safety checks (empty ledger, no live record,
  no legacy payment, migration head) BEFORE taking its EXCLUSIVE locks.
  Reproduced with the pre-fix script (rollback-concurrency.sh S0, S4c):
    S0  a receipt in flight when the rollback started: the check did not see it (uncommitted), the lock waited, the
        receipt committed, and the rollback then dropped billing_receipts -- a COMMITTED FINANCIAL RECORD WAS LOST.
    S4c a LIVE enablement in flight: same sequence -- the live record was dropped with the table.
  A second defect appeared when testing the fix: with EXCLUSIVE locks, the later DROP statements must upgrade to
  ACCESS EXCLUSIVE. A writer that was already waiting on the rollback while holding a read lock produced a DEADLOCK
  (first S2 run). Postgres aborted the rollback -- safe, nothing changed -- but under concurrent use it could not
  complete.
