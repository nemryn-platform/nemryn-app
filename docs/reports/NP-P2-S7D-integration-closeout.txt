5. DEFECTS AND OBSERVATIONS
======================================================================================================================
D-1 (legitimate, pre-existing Core, severity B — not fixed here, verification-only phase)
    Completed Trip Detail shows "Driver: Unassigned" and "Vehicle: —". Cause: src/lib/operations/trip-detail.ts:297
    shows only the ACTIVE assignment; completing a trip ends the assignment (trip_assignments.ended_at set), so the
    header loses who drove. Data is intact (assignment row kept; Proof of Service shows driver + vehicle). Fix: fall
    back to the last ended assignment for completed trips. Matters for billing review and customer disputes.
O-1 (minor UX, C) Opportunity "Move to stage" picker starts on "New inquiry" whatever the current stage (it is a target
    picker; the current stage is the badge). Could pre-select the next stage.
O-2 (demo note) A request created from an opportunity has no passenger linked; staff link / create the passenger
    before accepting. Intended Core behaviour; mention it in the demo.
O-3 (test hygiene, not product) The S7-B public E2E's fixture cleanup cannot delete inquiries that a previous run
    converted (opportunity FK). Run order / a reset avoids it; documented here, no change made.
No security defect found.

7. 10-MINUTE AGENCY DEMO SCRIPT ("Nemryn Complete")
======================================================================================================================
Audience: owner / dispatcher of a small NEMT company. Plain language. Fictional agency "Fictional Org A".
Setup (before the call, demo workspace only): Plus active; a published inquiry form; one fresh inquiry submitted from
the public link (e.g. with ?utm_source=google); one passenger record; one active driver + vehicle; driver signed in on
a phone (or a second browser at phone size). Never use real customer data.

0:00  The problem (30s)
      "Leads come in by phone, website and referrals; quotes live in spreadsheets; then dispatch retypes everything.
       Nemryn keeps it in one place: Growth wins the ride, Core runs it."
0:30  Core at a glance — Operations Brief (1 min)                                   [d01]
      Today's trips, what needs attention, tomorrow's readiness. "This is where your dispatcher starts the day."
1:30  A new inquiry arrives — website form (1 min)                                  [d03, d04, d05]
      Show the short public form on the agency's site ("questions, not bookings"). Back in Nemryn: Growth → Inquiries
      shows a badge. Open it: what they asked, who it's for, and that it came from a Google ad.            [d06]
2:30  Turn it into a customer — Convert (1 min)                                     [d07, d08]
      One click creates the contact and a sales opportunity. Point out: consent is only recorded for boxes the person
      actually ticked; "Do not contact" customers get a warning. Nothing is sent automatically.
3:30  Follow-up — Pipeline, contact, task (1 min)                                   [d13, d09, d22]
      Move the opportunity to Contacted → Qualified with a note; add "Send quote tomorrow" as a task. Show the contact
      timeline.
4:30  The quote (1.5 min)                                                           [d10, d11, d12]
      Create a quote (base fare, mileage, wheelchair assistance). Revise it — the old version is kept as superseded.
      Mark sent, then record "accepted" when the family says yes. Show the printable quote and the quotes list.
      Say clearly: "You send it the way you do today (print, PDF, email from your own mailbox); Nemryn records it."
6:00  Book it — hand off to operations (1 min)                                      [d09 → d14]
      "Create transportation request" turns the won opportunity into ONE pending request in Core. Dispatch decides:
      link or add the passenger (Passenger needed), then Accept. Nothing is booked automatically.
7:00  Schedule and dispatch (1 min)                                                 [d15, d16, d17]
      Create the trip from the request, assign driver and vehicle, see it on the dispatch board.
8:00  The driver's phone (1 min)                                                    [d18, d19]
      Driver sees the trip, navigates, taps arrived / passenger onboard / complete. Wheelchair and instructions show.
9:00  Proof the ride happened (40s)                                                 [d21]
      Proof of Service → Today: the completed trip with driver and vehicle, ready for review before billing.
      (Avoid opening the completed Trip Detail header until D-1 is fixed, or explain it.)
9:40  Wrap-up (20s)
      "Inquiry to completed ride, one system. Next on our roadmap: private-pay invoicing and payment, and facility
       referral management." Do NOT claim invoicing, online payment, quote e-mailing or customer e-signature.

Workarounds / not available during the demo

- Quote delivery and acceptance are staff-recorded; billing/payment not available (Billing is a placeholder).
- Handed-off request needs the passenger linked/created before accepting (intended).
- Completed Trip Detail header shows "Unassigned" (D-1) — use Proof of Service for the completed record.

     private-pay workflow inside Nemryn.
  A  Record payments against invoices (manual: cash / check / card-on-file reference) and outstanding balance.
  C  Online card payment collection (payment provider integration) — after manual payment recording is validated.
  C  Insurance / broker (Medicaid) billing — separate product decision.
Manage repeat customers
  —  Largely working: contact history, rebooking from a contact (existing), Standing Orders for recurring care.
  C  Customer-level price lists / saved rates.
