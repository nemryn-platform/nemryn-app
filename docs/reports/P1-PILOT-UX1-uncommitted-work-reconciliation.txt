  D1 (SECURITY, fixed) -- CSV / spreadsheet formula injection in the Day Sheet export.
     csvField (src/lib/operations/day-sheet-core.ts, original lines 79-82) quoted per RFC 4180 but did not
     neutralize cells beginning with = + - @ TAB CR. Attack path, verified in source: public website Request text
     prefills New Trip (src/components/operations/new-trip/NewTripForm.tsx:128,158) -> trips.pickup_description
     (src/app/operations/trips/new/actions.ts:57,176) -> Day Sheet CSV; passenger names can also come from the
     public request's requested_passenger_name prefill. A hostile submitter could plant e.g.
     =HYPERLINK("http://attacker","Click") that becomes a live formula when a dispatcher opens the CSV in
     Excel / Sheets. Severity: medium (requires a dispatcher to accept the text and open the CSV; no server impact).
