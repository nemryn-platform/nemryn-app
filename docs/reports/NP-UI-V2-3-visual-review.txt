3. OUTSTANDING ITEMS
--------------------
3.1 Website Requests (s12, s12a–c) render "This page couldn't be loaded" on the LOCAL production build, before and
    after V2-3.
    - Cause: src/lib/app-url.ts deliberately refuses to build application links from a loopback origin
      (http://localhost:3100) when NODE_ENV=production. This is a safety guard, not a defect.
    - V2-3 changed only these pages' D-14 copy and the shared Settings frame.
    - Their visual result could not be captured locally.
    - Verify on a non-loopback host (preview / production read-only) before approval.
3.2 Plainer screens, functionally unchanged:
    - the Growth Agent page (g12);
    - the Growth create / edit forms: New opportunity, New contact, and their edit pages.
    They use v2 tokens and controls, but are not yet in the raised-panel / icon-header anatomy. They are candidates for
    V2-4's integrated pass.
3.3 No lapsed ("Expired") quote is in the fixture.
    - The quote immutability trigger correctly refused moving a sent quote's valid-until date into the past.
    - The Expired tone is covered by the Quotes strict suites, not by these captures.
3.4 The five Growth Dashboard tiles wrap their labels to two lines at 1440. This is acceptable, but the Overview uses
    four tiles.
