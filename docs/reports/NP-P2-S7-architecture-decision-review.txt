EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
- P2-S7 has been designed before: product spec §4.2, domain model §13, access model §6b, tests T23–T26, PD-12 and
  PD-16. The design is sound in shape and remains the right foundation:
  - a separate Plus public boundary, three new tables and service-role-only public RPCs;
  - a staff triage inbox;
  - staff-reviewed conversion into the existing Contact + private-pay Opportunity;
  - Core intake untouched.
- It predates the RELEASED S6 consent ledger and several later precedents. Eight corrections are needed (spec §9).
  The important ones:
  1. Consent. The old design required a "follow-up" checkbox to submit and "copied the booleans" to the ledger. The
     deployed ledger is per (channel, purpose), bound to an address digest, and DNC-first.
     Recommended: both boxes optional and unticked. Conversion writes ledger rows only for ticked purposes, only for
     channels whose address matches the contact, voice (never SMS) for phone, and nothing at all for a do-not-contact
     contact.
  2. Spam controls. Core's form deliberately rejected a honeypot. The old S7 design used honeypot + fill time.
     Recommended: Core's proven controls (closed schema, publication, durable rate limit, uniform responses) plus a
     new server-side kill switch that defaults OFF.
  3. Navigation. The brief wants Growth → Enquiries; the old design hid the inbox inside the Pipeline.
  4. Enum alignment: `booking_interest` → `booking`, the same set as opportunities. `spam` becomes a dismiss reason.
- Everything else is reused:
  - entitlement helpers (`_plus_entitlement_state`, `_require_plus_write`, `_require_plus_admin_write`, `plus_can_read`);
  - contact matching (`_plus_contact_matches`);
  - contact / opportunity creation rules;
  - the consent ledger internals;
  - Core's public-form architecture (key format, hosted + iframe embed + loader, Origin rule, body cap, rate-limit
    hashing, uniform 404s, headers);
  - the DataTable navigable-row pattern;
  - Brand Lock v2 components.
- Four reviewable slices: S7-A (data + RLS), S7-B (public form + submission API), S7-C (inbox + conversion),
  S7-D (integrated verification + closeout). Each S7-A migration follows the owner's manual migration-before-merge
  workflow. The NP-RELEASE-01C gate stays paused.

PHASE 2 — PRODUCT DESIGN (detail: spec §2–§6)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Form configuration
   - Settings → Nemryn Plus → Inquiry form, Organization Admin only, effective `write`, active organization.
   - One form per organization.
   - Fields: headline (≤ 120), intro (≤ 500), offered services (a subset of the organization's Core service
     offerings; may be empty).
   - Draft → Published → Disabled. The key is stable across edits and re-publishes.
   - Edits to a published form apply live after a confirmation. `config_version` is recorded on every inquiry.
   - Outputs: the hosted link, the embed snippet, an iframe fallback, and builder guidance reused from Core D2.

B. Public submission experience
   - Hosted /inquiry/<key> and embed /embed/inquiry/<key> (loader /embed/inquiry-form.js).
   - The same design system as the Core form: organization name shown, "Powered by Nemryn".
   - Required fields: name; phone and/or email; question type; question (≤ 500).
   - Optional fields: preferred reply method, who is asking, service (from the offered list), coarse timeframe.
   - Two unticked, optional consent boxes.
   - A "Please don't include medical details" notice.
   - The success message promises a reply only: no booking, no reference number.
   - An unavailable form shows the neutral "This form isn't available".

C. Growth → Inquiries inbox
   - Tabs New / Converted / Dismissed / All, plus search, bounded pages and full-row navigation.
   - A "new" count on the nav item and on the Dashboard.

D. Inquiry detail and lifecycle
   - new → converted (final), new → dismissed (with a reason), dismissed → new (reopen).
   - Every transition uses a version token, re-checks Plus write, and is audited.
   - Read-only viewers see no actions.

E. Duplicates
   - An idempotency key per form render makes a replay return the same row.
   - Other duplicates are only HINTED on the detail page: matching contacts (`_plus_contact_matches`) and other open
     inquiries with the same email or phone digits.
   - Nothing auto-merges or auto-dismisses. Staff choose "Dismiss → duplicate" or "Convert → link existing contact".

F. Conversion
   - One transaction: create or link a Contact.
     - Linking never overwrites contact fields; it optionally fills blank phone / email.
   - Create one private-pay Opportunity: stage new_inquiry, source web_inquiry, inquiry_id, owner = the converter by
     default.
   - Never a Core Request, Passenger or Trip.

G. Dismissal and history
   - Dismiss reasons: spam / duplicate / not relevant / out of area / other (a note is required for "other").
   - The inquiry row keeps triaged_by / triaged_at.
   - audit_events: plus_inquiry_received is NOT written, to avoid an audit row per anonymous submission; the inquiry
     row is the evidence. These ARE written: plus_inquiry_converted, _dismissed, _reopened and the form
     publish / disable / save actions.
   - Conversion adds one `inquiry_converted` activity on the new opportunity.

H. Consent and DNC
   - The ticks are stored on the inquiry as "what the person ticked". They become ledger rows only at conversion,
     per the rules in the executive summary: ticked purposes only, matching addresses only, voice never SMS, nothing
     for a DNC contact.
   - captured_via web_form, captured_at = received_at, text version, inquiry_id.
   - Converting to a do-not-contact contact needs a server-enforced acknowledgement, and then writes no consent.
   - A submission never changes DNC.

I. Access and tenancy
   - Tenant comes from the published key only.
   - Every staff read is plus_can_read; every write is _require_plus_write; publishing is _require_plus_admin_write.
   - Composite foreign keys everywhere. Platform Admin without a membership sees nothing.

J. Agencies without repository access
   - The hosted link: paste it into a menu, button, email signature or ad.
   - The embed snippet for WordPress / Wix / Squarespace / Webflow / custom HTML, with an iframe fallback where
     scripts are stripped. Copy is reused from Core D2.
   - No plugin, app or API key to manage.
   - The vanity host (request.nemryn.com) and a developer API are later options (OD-S7-13 / OD-S7-14).

Data foundation assessment (the previously proposed tables):
- plus_inquiry_forms — APPROPRIATE. Changes:
  - `id` PK + unique(organization_id), as Core D2 does;
  - add config_version and a version token;
  - the key is `inq_` + 32 hex.
- plus_inquiries — APPROPRIATE. Changes:
  - inquiry_type aligned to the opportunity set;
  - `spam` → dismiss reason;
  - postal_code removed;
  - preferred_reply added;
  - both consent booleans default false and optional;
  - form_id + form_config_version added;
  - a version token added;
  - idempotency key hidden by column privilege.
- plus_inquiry_rate_limit_events — APPROPRIATE as proposed (it mirrors Core).
- Additive columns: plus_opportunities.inquiry_id and plus_communication_consents.inquiry_id (as proposed). Plus the
  named-constraint replacements: captured_via += web_form, the text-version rule, and activity_type +=
  inquiry_converted.
- Rejected alternatives:
  - reusing website_request_forms or publications (Core-owned, booking-shaped);
  - writing submissions straight into plus_contacts (PD-12: an inbox first);
  - a generic "public forms" table shared with Core (it would couple a Core hardened path to Plus).

PHASE 3 — SECURITY AND PRIVACY DESIGN
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Deny-by-default RLS
   - Every new table has RLS on.
   - The forms and inquiries tables: a select policy via plus_can_read and SELECT for authenticated only (column
     privileges hide idempotency_key).
   - The rate-limit table: no policy and no grants at all.
   - No INSERT / UPDATE / DELETE for any client role (T9).
2. Tenant isolation
   - The public org comes only from a published key inside the service-role RPC; the body cannot name an
     organization, form, contact or status (closed key set).
   - Staff RPCs use the org parameter and has_org_role.
   - Composite FKs make a cross-tenant link a FK violation (T12 pattern).
3. Anonymous submission
   - Route handler → service-role RPCs only. Direct anon / authenticated EXECUTE gives 42501 (T23).
   - Uniform 404 for unknown / draft / disabled keys, a suspended organization, a non-write Plus state, and the kill
     switch off.
4. No service-role exposure
   - The client is only in `server-only` modules (a bundler build error otherwise); no NEXT_PUBLIC variable.
   - The client's caller-list comment is corrected.
5. Rate limiting, spam and replay
   - Durable atomic limits: 5 / client / h and 60 / form / h, proposed.
   - 8 KB cap; Origin limited to Nemryn's own origins; closed schema; idempotency key unique per org.
   - Minimal responses.
   - No honeypot (Core D2 precedent) and no challenge vendor in v1 (PD-16a). Revisit before real data, using
     synthetic and early abuse data.
   - The kill switch PLUS_INQUIRY_SUBMISSIONS defaults "off".
6. Validation and errors
   - Pure core validation mirrors the SQL CHECKs; the database is authoritative.
   - Public responses: {"ok":true}, generic 400 / 429, or uniform 404 (no ids, reasons or limits).
   - Staff RPCs use ZW001–ZW006.
7. Deduplication
   - Idempotent replay; hint-only matching; no automatic merge. Conversion is unique per inquiry (status guard + a
     unique inquiry_id on opportunities) and concurrency-tested.
8. Least privilege
   - READ grant: view. WRITE grant: triage. Admin: publish.
   - No Driver, no inactive member, no Platform Admin without a membership.
9. Entitlements
   - write: full. read_only: Admin read, no writes (ZW001), form 404. locked / none: no access, form 404.
   - Core is unaffected in every state (T8).
10. No implicit consent
   - Submission creates no ledger row. Ticks are optional and unticked.
   - Consent is recorded only by a named staff member at conversion, as web_form evidence with a text version.
   - A follow-up tick never implies marketing.
11. DNC precedence
   - A DNC contact gets no consent rows; conversion needs an acknowledgement; DNC is never changed by an inquiry.
   - The ledger's own ZW004 refusal still guards it.
12. Data minimisation
   - No address, postal code, date of birth, diagnosis, insurance, appointment date or attachments.
   - Coarse timeframe only; ≤ 500-character question with a no-medical-details notice.
   - No tracking scripts on /inquiry or /embed (PD-15). No raw IP stored.
13. Retention, deletion and audit
   - No purge until PD-11. Proposals: rate-limit rows 30 days; dismissed inquiries 90 days; converted inquiries with
     the contact.
   - Contact erasure must also redact linked inquiries and digests (ZD-232).
   - Audit logs ids only, never question text.
   - Real data stays blocked by PD-10 / PD-11 (OD-S7-16).
