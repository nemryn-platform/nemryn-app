2. EMERGENCY CORRECTIONS -- ZD-287 (EC-1 to EC-6)
======================================================================================================================
  Approved principles
    EC-1 No general-purpose temporary billing enablement to correct records in a disabled organization. Default-deny
         stays. (This withdraws the interim "open a window" procedure of NP-FIN-P1G-implementation.txt §2.1.)
    EC-2 A future correction-only capability is scoped by organization, operation, record and expiry.
    EC-3 It requires Platform Admin approval and complete audit evidence: request, approval, the correction, expiry.
    EC-4 Corrections of sensitive live data need an additional approval, unless an explicitly approved emergency
         exception exists.
    EC-5 The full feature is not built in P1G.
    EC-6 Before the first LIVE organization is enabled, this capability or a separately approved equivalent control
         must exist. This is a blocker for live enablement, not for the synthetic release.
  Remaining implementation decisions (to be taken when the capability is planned)
    - who approves, and whether a second person must confirm (two-person rule);
    - maximum expiry;
    - the exact operation list (candidates: void, unapply, refund correction, receipt reversal);
    - how the scoped authorization is presented to the tenant;
    - the criteria and the approver of an emergency exception (EC-4).
  Design direction already sketched (implementation report §2.2): a correction-only authorization row (org,
  operation, record, expiry, approver) that the four correction RPCs consume. The gate admits only that write, only
  before expiry.
