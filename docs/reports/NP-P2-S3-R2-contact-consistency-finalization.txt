     (ZW004 'linked_requests').

  - no opportunity change and no updated_at bump;

5. THE CORRECTION WORKFLOW -- DEMONSTRATED WITH EXISTING RPCs, UI AND CONSTRAINTS
======================================================================
Documented flow (domain model section 10; mutation API):
  1. Open each linked Request in Operations (Request detail -> Growth panel).
  2. Use "Correct link".
  3. Record the reason (required, 3-500; health details discouraged by the help text).
  4. Remove the opportunity from that Request's link (choose the contact and "No opportunity"), or move the link to
     another suitable existing opportunity of the chosen contact.
  5. When no Request remains linked to the original opportunity, change its primary contact (opportunity edit form).
  6. If appropriate, re-link Requests explicitly with "Correct link". With the opportunity selected, the contact
     dimension follows the opportunity's NEW primary contact.

  - unlink with a reason -> changed (contact kept, opportunity removed);
  - contact change -> allowed;
  - re-link to the opportunity with a reason -> the attribution is (new contact, opportunity);
  - the pair is consistent again; link_method still linked_existing_request.
- SQL OB8: truthful history. Two attribution_changed entries carry their reasons; two
  plus_request_attribution_changed audit rows; exactly one plus_opportunity_updated audit row with 'contact', written
  only after the Request was unlinked.
- The UI can complete every step. The "Correct link" picker offers "No opportunity" for any chosen contact, and lists
  the chosen contact's own opportunities, so step 6 lists the re-pointed opportunity under its new contact.
- A contact / opportunity mismatch cannot be saved at any step (ZW006 by _plus_resolve_attribution).
- No blocker was found, so no further fix was needed.
